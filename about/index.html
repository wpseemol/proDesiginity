Home / About

We're ProDesignity.

A design and development agency working with store owners, founders and brands who need their online presence to do real work.

Why we exist

Most brands hire three vendors and get four styles.

A store from one freelancer, a landing page from another, a promo video from a third. Each piece is fine on its own, and together they look like three different companies.

ProDesignity was set up to remove that gap. We handle the Shopify build, the website, the 3D product visuals and the animated video under one roof, so the brand you spent money on stays intact from the ad to the checkout button.

How we work

Fixed scope, plain language, no disappearing.

You get a written scope before anything starts, progress you can see at each stage, and a person who answers messages. We quote in advance and stick to it.

Most of our clients come from referrals and from our Facebook page, which tells you how we'd rather grow: by finishing what we start.

What we hold to

Three things we
don't negotiate.

Speed is a feature

A beautiful store that takes six seconds to load is a broken store. Performance is part of the build, not an upsell.

Findable by default

Every page we ship has proper titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap. Being invisible is not a design choice.

You own everything

Files, source, accounts and admin access are handed over at the end. No hostage situations.

Common questions

Before you message us.

Where are you based, and do you work with clients abroad?

We're based in Bangladesh and work with clients worldwide. Most communication happens over WhatsApp and email, and we schedule calls around your timezone.

How long does a Shopify store take?

A customized theme build with your products set up usually runs one to three weeks. A fully custom design with bespoke sections takes longer. You'll get a specific timeline in the quote before anything begins.

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we quote per project rather than publishing a rate card. Send us the requirements and a budget range and we'll tell you honestly what's possible within it.

Can you do just the video, or just the website?

Yes. The services are bundled when it helps you and separate when it doesn't. Plenty of clients come to us only for animation or only for a store fix.

What happens after launch?

You get the files and full admin access. We stay available for updates, fixes and new sections — either ad hoc or on a monthly arrangement.
